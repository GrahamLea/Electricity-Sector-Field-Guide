# Battery Electric Vehicle


## Synonyms
- Fully electric vehicle
- Full EV
- All-electric vehicle
- Pure electric vehicle

## Abbreviations
- BEV


## Summary

An [electric vehicle] that only uses an electric drivetrain, with no fuel-powered engine.


## More

An advantage commonly cited for BEVs is that they have "zero tailpipe emissions", in
contrast to [hybrid electric vehicle]s, so are sometimes also categorised as ZEVs,
along with hydrogen fuel cell electric vehicles.
