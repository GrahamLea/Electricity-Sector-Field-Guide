# Regenerative Braking

## Synonyms
- Regen braking
- KERS

## Summary

A method of [energy] recovery used to slow vehicles down by converting their kinetic energy
into another form, typically electrical energy by using an [electric motor] as a generator. 
The energy is most typically stored for use in the same vehicle, though it can also be
used directly by other systems in the vehicle,
or delivered into a shared power network.


## More

Regenerative braking is present in almost all modern road [electric vehicle]s, and is a
crucial factor in extending the range of EVs, especially in stop-start traffic.
It's also very widely applied in electric rail transport.

"Regenerative braking" is sometimes used to refer to a feature of EVs where
releasing the accelerator actively starts slowing the car rather than coasting.
Technically, though, that feature is more accurately described as "one-pedal driving",
while regenerative braking is the feature of the car's internals that 
might be activated by releasing the accelerator,
but could instead rely on the brake pedal being pushed.

Regenerative braking features in Formula One cars, 
where it is typically called KERS (kinetic energy recovery system).
