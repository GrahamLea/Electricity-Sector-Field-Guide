# Hybrid Electric Vehicle

## Abbreviations
- HEV


## Summary

An [electric vehicle] that has both an [electric motor] (or motors) and a fuel-based engine,
using e.g. petrol (gasoline) or diesel.


## More

Some hybrids can charge their [battery] from an external power source ([EVSE]), 
making them [plug-in hybrid electric vehicle]s.
Others source electrical [energy] only from [regenerative braking] 
or from using their fuel engine to charge the battery (or [supercapacitor]) via an alternator.

HEVs are often classified as "full hybrid", where the electric motor can propel the
vehicle independently, 
or "mild", where the electric motor only functions in support of the fuel engine.
There are also a variety of possible powertrain configurations, 
including the fuel engine being used solely to power the electric motor, 
or solely to charge the battery (known as extended-range EV (EREV)).

All current Formula One cars are HEVs (but not PHEVs).
