# Controlled Load

## Summary

In Australian energy markets, a device controlled remotely by a [DNSP]. 
Also used as an adjective for the circuit the device is wired into, 
the metering channel measuring that circuit, 
and the tariff that determines its pricing (at a discounted rate).


## More

The most common type of device connected to these circuits is residential electric hot
water storage systems, with this arrangement colloquially known as "off-peak hot water".

Controlled Load tariffs guarantee that controlled load devices receive a certain number 
of hours of running time per day, but without contracting exact time periods, allowing 
the operator to vary the running time of the loads as part of [demand management].

See also [controllable loads].
